# 🚀 Nexora AI

Nexora AI is a platform dedicated to helping students, professionals, and creators discover the best AI tools, productivity resources, technology guides, and practical tutorials.

---

## 🌟 Vision

To build one of the most trusted free AI resource platforms on the internet.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Provide high-quality AI tool recommendations, tutorials, productivity tips, and technology resources that help people learn faster and work smarter.
